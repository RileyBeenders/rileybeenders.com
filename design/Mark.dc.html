The Mark
One stroke, top to bottom
The stem is its own mark. Both bowls of the B are drawn as a single continuous curve that starts at the top of the stem and finishes at the bottom — never lifting. The gradient runs navy to blue along that travel, so the eye follows the flow.
Travel
Top of stem → out and back → out further and back → base. Wider on the second pass, the way a B’s lower bowl always is.
