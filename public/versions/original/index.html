Summary

I’m an electromechanical engineer and R&D leader specializing in product development, manufacturing and industrial automation. That same curiosity extends beyond my professional work — I love traveling the world, rebuilding cars, replicating movie props and finding creative ways to automate the repetitive parts of my everyday life. RileyBeenders.com is another extension of my curiosity — a platform I built to house my experience, skills, and projects in one place, create focused applications for ambitious opportunities, and demonstrate the full range of what I can offer.
