About This Site

This site was created as a portfolio website designed to complement a traditional resume. The PDF remains the official application document, while the website serves as an interactive "evidence of excellence" layer that demonstrates the work behind the claims on the resume.

At rest, the site stays readable as a conventional resume. Its goal is to preserve the clarity and familiarity of a standard resume while giving visitors a more engaging way to explore projects, supporting visuals, and professional impact.
