01  Summary

I'm an electro-mechanical engineer and a leader specializing in product development, manufacturing and industrial automation, as well as someone who's quick to pick up, apply, and integrate new technologies into real-world systems. I unite mechanical design, electronics, controls, software, and manufacturing to develop reliable, practical products and systems built for real-world use.
