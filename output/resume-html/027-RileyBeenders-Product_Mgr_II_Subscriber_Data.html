ExtrusionStudio | Plant measurement and production data system, Proteor
September 2025 - Present

Python, asyncio, Modbus TCP, Starlette, SQLite, Server-Sent Events, Raspberry Pi

Problem: Each of three extrusion lines had a tri-axial laser gauge, and the only record of a spool's diameter was whatever an operator wrote down. Production was speeding up, and the plant needed every reading captured, every spool judged against its tolerance, and the result visible to the operator, the packing bench, and management at once.

  • Runs one asyncio poller and Modbus TCP session per line, reading every line concurrently at 20 Hz so a powered-down line never stalls a healthy one; the poll rate is per-line configuration with a floor, because the right value depends on the PLC's response time.
  • Added a calibration phase that times the filament from the laser to the spool, a delay of one to two minutes through the accumulator, so each reading and alarm lands on the spool it is actually wound on rather than the one under the laser.
  • Logs every spool to its own CSV and a SQLite run table with a serial; the packing bench approves, inspects, or rejects each spool with the reasons recorded on its serial, and an approved spool gets an HTML certificate.
  • Split the interface into a view per audience (a projector view for the floor, a touch screen per line, the packing bench, and a production statistics TV for management), streamed live over Server-Sent Events.
